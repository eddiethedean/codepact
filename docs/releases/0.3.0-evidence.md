## Concurrent resource evidence

CS01's candidate scope is one-task coroutine analysis: coroutine creation
retains captured resources without executing the body or applying cleanup;
resolved local coroutine bodies execute only at a direct `await`. Repeated
awaits, unresolved awaitables and returned or scheduled work remain visible
boundaries. At most 128 pending coroutine records are retained per flow state;
exhaustion widens affected captures and duties instead of inferring completion.
The exact `contextlib.closing` and `contextlib.aclosing` models are selected for
CPython 3.11–3.13. The controlled runtime conformance cases also ran on 3.14,
but 3.14 model selection remains unsupported; interpreter patch versions and
source hashes are listed in the [conformance record](../planning/phases/0.3/contextlib-conformance.md).

The V03-17–20 and V03-26 mappings cover creation versus execution, captured
Owned/Borrowed duties, cancellation before coroutine entry, cancellation
during use and awaited cleanup, async context entry and exit, failed entry,
LIFO unwind, suppression uncertainty, nested awaited finalizers, and combined
coroutine/context-exit budget exhaustion. V03-22/23 cover fresh/reused parity
after coroutine, cancellation, model and budget edits, including recovery
after exhaustion. Controlled `closing`/`aclosing` runtime cases pass on
CPython 3.11–3.14; these cases validate the listed runtime behavior and do not
expand the analyzer's 3.11–3.13 model range.

TaskGroup and other task scheduling or joining, detached/shielded work,
async-generator finalization, thread/process handoffs, and task completion
inference remain unsupported and retain uncertainty. The candidate makes no
race-freedom, deadlock-freedom, or general task-lifetime guarantee; see the
[async specification](../planning/phases/0.3/specification.md#intratask-async-and-cs01),
[concurrency plan](../planning/concurrency.md), and [user limitations](../limitations.md).
These mappings and runtime checks are implementation evidence only. CS01 and
the related V03 cases remain pending maintainer qualification.

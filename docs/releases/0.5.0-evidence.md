### Candidate performance replay

The optimized `benchmark_phase05` executable used the exact candidate source,
Rust 1.96.0, and Python source target 3.11 on macOS arm64. Timings are medians
of seven samples in milliseconds. Peak RSS is the whole child process in
MiB, combined for the two legacy scopes. The inherited baseline and the
original preview results are in the [performance record](../planning/phases/0.5/performance.md).

| Workload | Scope | Cold ms | Warm ms | Edit ms | Report ms | Peak RSS MiB |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 250 borrowed lifecycle calls | Default | 56.036 | 0.012 | 56.652 | 0.562 | 32.62 combined |
| 250 borrowed lifecycle calls | Ownership | 77.078 | 0.012 | 76.579 | 0.561 | 32.62 combined |
| 4,095 aliases | Ownership | 16.852 | 0.014 | 16.641 | 2.732 | 32.59 |
| 96 recursive transfers | Ownership | 80.234 | 0.011 | 79.682 | 0.329 | 12.59 |
| 128 children across nested groups | Ownership and TaskGroup | 18.050 | 0.009 | 18.550 | 0.289 | 9.61 |

The task workload's cold median is 11% above the earlier 16.239 ms sample
and its peak RSS is 0.38 MiB higher. The review fixes retain caller capture
evidence across resolved calls and distinguish TaskGroup exception outcomes.
The timings include that work and do not isolate its individual cost.
The unchanged-session checks compared the full reports and passed.

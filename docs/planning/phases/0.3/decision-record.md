# 0.3 semantic decision record

Status: **Draft; no semantic decision has been accepted.** This record makes
the CL01 choices reviewable in one place. `eddiethedean` may review, validate
and accept every choice alone; no additional validator is required. Proposed
defaults and automated evidence do not count as acceptance or pass a gate.

For each checkpoint, record the accepted option, considered alternative,
evidence revision and V03 cases, limitations, reconsideration trigger, and
maintainer/date. Keep the decision Pending until the maintainer records those
fields. A release decision is separate from these semantic decisions.

| Duty origin and delegation | Create one duty after successful local acquisition or explicit `Owned` entry. `Borrowed` and unqualified parameters create no incoming duty.
